Add document-level `options.structureOptions: false` to omit a type from autogenerated structure without removing its schema, contextual lists, references, templates, or creation options. Existing singleton restrictions still apply, and groups containing only hidden types are omitted.

Add `structure.keepPanesOnCreate` to forward pane retention during document creation to Sanity. Omission preserves Sanity's default, and explicit `false` is retained.
